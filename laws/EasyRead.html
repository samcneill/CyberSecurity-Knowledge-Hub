AU/NZ Cybersecurity Laws

Easy Read

What are laws?

Laws are rules.

Everyone must follow the rules.

What is cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity means staying safe online.

It means keeping your information safe.

It means protecting your computer and phone.

What are cybersecurity laws?

Cybersecurity laws are rules about staying safe online.

These laws help protect you.

These laws help protect your information.

Privacy Act

This is a law in Australia.

This is a law in New Zealand.

This law says:

Companies must keep your information safe.

Companies must tell you if someone steals your information.

You can ask what information a company has about you.

What is personal information?

Personal information is information about you.

This includes:

Your name

Your address

Your phone number

Your email address

Your bank details

Data Breach Rules

A data breach means someone stole information.

Or someone lost information.

Companies must tell you if this happens.

They must tell you what information was lost.

They must tell you how they will fix it.

They must tell you how to stay safe.

What happens if someone breaks the law?

Breaking the law has consequences.

This means something bad will happen.

They might have to pay money.

They might have to go to court.

They must fix the problem.

Critical Infrastructure Law

Critical infrastructure means important things.

Things that everyone needs.

This law protects:

Electricity

Water

Hospitals

Buses and trains

Internet and phones

This law keeps these things safe from cyber attacks.

How to stay safe online

You can do things to stay safe.

Here are some easy ways:

Use strong passwords

Use letters, numbers, and symbols.

Example: MyDog123!

Keep passwords secret

Do not tell anyone your password.

Do not write it down where others can see.

Be careful what you share

Think before you post online.

Do not share private information.

Update your devices

Update your computer regularly.

Update your phone regularly.

Updates keep you safe.

Be careful with emails

Do not open emails from strangers.

Do not click on strange links.

Where to get help

You can get help if you need it.

There are people who can help you.

Australian Cyber Security Centre

Phone: 1300 CYBER1

Or: 1300 292 371

They can help if you have a cyber problem.

CERT NZ (New Zealand)

Website: www.cert.govt.nz

They help people in New Zealand.

They help with cyber security problems.

Scamwatch

Website: www.scamwatch.gov.au

Report scams here.

A scam is when someone tries to trick you.

Office of the Australian Information Commissioner

Website: www.oaic.gov.au

They help with privacy problems.

Call them if a company does not protect your information.

Remember

Laws are there to protect you.

You have rights.

You can ask for help.

Stay safe online!
